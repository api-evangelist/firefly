## The short answer for agents

Firefly publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/Swagger document, no GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no
webhooks and no SDK on any package registry. Do not attempt to construct calls against
Firefly — there is no published contract to call. Buying Firefly media programmatically
is done through third-party DOOH supply-side platforms, not through a Firefly endpoint.

## How Firefly is actually integrated with

- Programmatic supply is transacted through third-party DOOH SSPs. Firefly's own
  [advertising solutions page](https://www.fireflyon.com/advertising-solutions) names
  Place Exchange, Vistar Media, Hivestack and Broadsign, and describes Programmatic
  Guaranteed and open-marketplace deals. Firefly has also announced a VIOOH integration.
  The OpenRTB contract a buyer integrates against belongs to those platforms.
- Firefly operates a "Firefly Mediation Server" that sends bid requests to Vistar, Place
  Exchange, Hivestack, Adomni and Magnite and runs a header-bidding auction over the
  responses. It is an internal server-to-server component; no schema or endpoint for it
  is published.
- Programmatic and technical inquiries go to programmatic@fireflyon.com.
